Who I am

Hello! I'm AllChemi, a 2D and 3D artist using softwares such as Blender, Krita, and Substance Painter. I'm still building up my portfolio and skills, but I very much enjoy creating models. I work fast depending on the complexity of the task, and I'm always looking forward to improving and learning with every model I render.
